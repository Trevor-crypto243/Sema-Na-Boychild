Why This Matters

Across Kenya and East Africa, many boys grow up navigating pressure, silence, and limited guidance. Without mentorship and safe spaces, potential turns into frustration. Sema Na Boychild exists to interrupt that cycle and build grounded, responsible, emotionally intelligent men.

Our Mentorship Journey

1. Emotional Development

Safe discussions that help boys understand emotions, mental health, and healthy masculinity.

2. Leadership Training

Practical sessions on responsibility, communication, and integrity within community settings.

3. Career & Purpose

Exposure to mentors, career pathways, and skill development to prepare boys for adulthood.

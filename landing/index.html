Why This Matters

Across Kenya and East Africa, many boys grow up navigating pressure, silence, and limited guidance. Without mentorship and safe spaces, potential turns into frustration. Sema Na Boychild exists to interrupt that cycle and build grounded, responsible, emotionally intelligent men.
